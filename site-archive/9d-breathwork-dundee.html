What Is 9D Breathwork?

A guided experience unlike anything you've tried before.

You lie down, put on headphones, and breathe.

What happens next is hard to describe — but most people leave feeling calmer, clearer and lighter than they have in years.

9D Breathwork combines conscious connected breathing with immersive soundscapes, binaural beats and a guiding voice — all through headphones while you lie on a mat. It works at a level that talking, thinking and willpower alone often can't reach.

People come to our Dundee sessions from across Tayside, Angus, Fife and Perthshire. Arbroath, Forfar, Perth, Kirkcaldy — the drive is worth it. Most leave having released something they didn't know they were still holding.

Sessions last around 2.5 hours in total — including a gentle opening circle and integration time. No experience needed. Just show up.

What to Expect

Simple to show up for. Powerful once you're in it.

No preparation required. No experience needed. Just turn up as you are.

Before your Dundee session

Wear comfortable, loose clothing you can breathe easily in. Avoid a heavy meal in the 2 hours beforehand. Bring a blanket or pillow if you'd like — we provide mats. Arrive 10 minutes early.

During the session

You'll lie down, put on headphones and breathe. You're guided the whole way through. Emotions, sensations and release are all completely normal — there's nothing you need to do except breathe and trust the process.

After the session

Most people feel calmer, lighter and deeply relaxed. Some feel energised — others need a quiet evening. Drink plenty of water, eat something nourishing, and give yourself space to integrate.

How long does it take?

Allow around 2.5 hours in total — a welcome circle, the breathwork journey itself, and integration time at the end. Integration is part of the process — please don't rush off straight after.

About 9D Breathwork

What is 9D Breathwork?

+

9D Breathwork is a guided breathwork experience that combines conscious connected breathing with immersive soundscapes, binaural beats and a guiding voice — all through headphones while you lie comfortably on a mat. It's designed to help you access and release things that talking alone often can't reach.

Do I need any experience?

+

Not at all. The majority of people at our Dundee sessions have never done breathwork before. You're guided the whole way through — there's nothing to prepare or understand in advance. Just show up.

Is 9D Breathwork safe?

+

Yes — 9D Breathwork is safe for the vast majority of people, and you remain in control throughout. For the full honest answer, read our guide: Is breathwork safe? Every participant completes an intake form before their session. If you have any health concerns, get in touch before booking and we'll have a conversation.

I have a health condition — can I still attend?

+

We assess each person individually through our intake form. Please fill it in honestly — the more we know, the better we can support you. We will never exclude someone without first having a conversation. Please also read our safety guide before booking.
